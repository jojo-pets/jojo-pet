Care guide

Keeping them happy

Four habits make the biggest difference for a guinea pig's health and mood.

🌾

Hay, always

Unlimited timothy hay is the foundation of the diet and keeps teeth worn down.

🥬

Fresh greens

Romaine, bell pepper, and parsley daily. Keep fruit as an occasional treat only.

🍊

Vitamin C

They cannot make their own. Provide it every day through food or supplements.

🏡

Room to move

At least 7.5 sq ft of floor space per pig, with hiding spots and safe chew toys.
